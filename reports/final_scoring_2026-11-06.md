# Final scoring (stage 5)

Issue #115, timeline 5.2, workflow.md §6.1, spec §6. Every frozen system on dev, Adversarial SQuAD and SQuAD-Shifts, with the official scorer. Nothing was fitted on these sets: readers reuse their validation temperature, and row 4 its validation λ and temperatures.

Scores are means over 3 seeds, as EM / F1. Adversarial scores are worst case over each question's versions. Intervals are 95% paired bootstrap over questions. The full tables, with spreads and the averages over all questions and originals, are in `reports/final_scoring/tables.md`.

**The systems.** Row 0: standard head. Row 1: joint head, MAP. Row 3: the same reader, MBR. Row 4: row 3 plus the separate verifier (ModernBERT-large), 3 verifier seeds on reader seed 0, so its contrast is against row 3 on that seed. Row 5: row 1 trained with AdversarialQA as well, reader only.

## Findings

1. **The verifier gains on dev and SQuAD-Shifts, after tying on validation.** Against row 3 on dev it gives +0.62 EM [+0.33, +0.91] and +0.26 F1 [+0.11, +0.41], and on the Shifts sets +0.73 to +2.11 EM and +0.32 to +1.29 F1, with every interval above 0. On validation, where λ and the temperature were fitted, the same contrast is a tie: +0.12 EM [−0.24, +0.47], −0.13 F1 [−0.32, +0.05]. Several references explain part of that: against dev's first reference alone, the gain is +0.38 EM [+0.06, +0.70] and +0.13 F1 [−0.05, +0.31].
2. **On NYT, about half of the verifier's EM gain is punctuation at the edge of an answer.** Against the reader on the same lists, it gains +1.74 EM on NYT. Of that, +0.84 comes from the 1.15% of questions where the two answers differ only by a curly quote or dash at an edge, which the scorer doesn't strip. On every other set those questions contribute at most +0.03 EM, so the gains there come from other changes.
3. **The verifier hurts F1 under attack.** On AddSent it gives −1.14 F1 [−2.08, −0.23] and on AddOneSent −0.84 [−1.70, −0.03]; both EM intervals include 0. We didn't test why.
4. **AdversarialQA training gains robustness and costs nothing elsewhere.** Row 5 against row 3 gives +3.13 EM [+1.87, +4.37] and +3.23 F1 [+2.12, +4.36] on AddSent, and +2.57 EM [+1.33, +3.83] and +2.62 F1 [+1.59, +3.71] on AddOneSent. It also gains on dev (+0.36 EM [+0.09, +0.64]; F1 +0.13 [−0.04, +0.31]) and on the Shifts sets (+0.56 to +1.52 EM). It tied on validation (−0.19 EM [−0.53, +0.16]).
5. **No system is best everywhere.** Row 4 has the highest EM on dev and SQuAD-Shifts, and row 5 the highest on Adversarial SQuAD. Rows 4 and 5 weren't contrasted directly, because row 4 stands on one reader seed, and no verifier was trained on row 5's reader.
6. **The joint head row gains on dev, but this isn't the head alone.** Row 1 against row 0 gives +0.49 EM [+0.21, +0.78] and +0.30 F1 [+0.14, +0.47] on dev, after a tie on validation. Row 1 also trained for 2 epochs rather than 1, and on evenly spread windows. Row 0*, which matches those, wasn't scored because its checkpoints can't be read from the account that ran this.
7. **MBR's validation gain doesn't carry over.** Row 3 against row 1 gives +0.18 EM + F1 [+0.04, +0.31] on validation, −0.04 [−0.17, +0.10] on dev, and it loses on reddit (−0.36 [−0.58, −0.14]) and amazon_reviews (−0.33 [−0.52, −0.14]).
8. **Dev's extra references explain why it scores above validation.** Row 0 scores 90.04 / 95.31 on dev, and 74.82 / 87.78 against the first reference alone, beside 76.89 / 89.62 on validation. This reproduces job 911434 (90.03 / 95.30, and 74.81 / 87.77; AGENTS.md).

## The decoding bug found during this scoring

**Row 4 was scored twice.** The first scoring carried a bug that lowered its NYT score by about 3.2 EM and turned its contrast with row 3 there from a gain into a loss. Both versions are below. Row 4's first scoring is in `reports/final_scoring/before_decode_fix/`, and its files can be regenerated by running `slurm/final_scoring.sbatch` at 8039d2c (jobs 1079267, 1079384, 1079395).

**The bug.** `decode.answer_groups` keeps the first candidate of each group of boundary variants, assuming candidates come most probable first. `top_k`'s do, but a verifier list has been sorted by position since 8355130 (28 Oct). So every answer decoded from a list took its group's earliest-starting variant, where the spec and the function's own docstring say the most probable one. The scorer ignores most such differences, such as a leading "the", but not a curly quote or dash at an edge, and NYT has many of those.

**How it was found, and why that matters.** Stage A's check showed 24.6% of reader-only answers differing between the lists and `predict`, and it was first dismissed as harmless boundary variants. Row 4's low NYT score then prompted the trace. So the fix was made after seeing a test score that looked wrong. A bug in row 4's favour might not have drawn the same check.

**Why the corrected numbers can be trusted.**

- The decision was sized on validation only, by simulating the fix, before any corrected test number was computed.
- It adds no parameter, and λ and the temperatures don't move. On validation, combined EM rises 0.03 and F1 is flat on each seed.
- The verifier's raw scores on the final sets are the same: identical on seeds 0 and 1, and within 0.03 on seed 2 (about one bf16 rounding step). All three seeds reproduce their rescored validation answers exactly.
- The reader through the lists now matches `predict`'s reader within 0.22 EM on every set, where it was 2.19 below on NYT, and answers that differ in any way fall from about a quarter to 1–2%. The remaining difference is expected, since lists hold 5 candidates and `predict` 10.
- Rows 0, 1, 3 and 5 don't go through this path. On validation's saved candidates, the fix changes none of the answers of rows 0, 1 and 3.

**What else it touched.** #102's and #112's validation numbers for row 4 rise by 0.03 EM and have been rescored here. #116's report keeps its story, with new numbers. #117's finding 2 was wrong: the verifier lowers the shifted share rather than raising it, and that report has been corrected. E2's decision stands: on the separate verifier's lists, the shared verifier on span lists moves about +0.01 EM against +0.03, and a list with one span per group has no variants to choose between. Any run decoded through `train_verifier` from position-sorted lists before this fix carries the bug. #109–#111's probably do, but their code isn't pushed, so that isn't checked.

## Limits

- All three row 4 runs share reader seed 0. Its intervals cover question sampling, and its spread covers only the verifier's seed.
- The gain split in finding 2 has no interval, and uses one rule for "edge punctuation": non-ASCII punctuation at either end.
- Adversarial scores are worst case over small groups, so seeds spread by up to about 1 point on AddSent.
- Row 5 has no verifier, so these numbers don't show whether the verifier's loss under attack survives AdversarialQA training.
